SQuote – Stock Tracker Widget Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-20

SQuote – Stock Tracker Widget is a free Android application that displays stock and market quotes in home-screen widgets.

Information stored on your device

SQuote stores widget settings locally on your device, including stock or market symbols, refresh-frequency settings, optional price-alert thresholds, and widget display preferences.

SQuote does not provide user accounts and does not maintain a personal user database.

Information sent over the network

To obtain quote information, SQuote sends the requested stock or market symbol to Yahoo Finance's quote service.

SQuote does not intentionally send your name, email address, contacts, location, account credentials, payment information, or advertising identifier.

Normal Internet connection information, such as an IP address and technical request metadata, may be visible to Yahoo Finance or network providers. Their handling of that information is governed by their own privacy policy and terms.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

SQuote does not contain advertising, user accounts, behavioral analytics, or cross-application tracking. The developer does not receive a list of users and does not know which individuals use the application or how they use it.

Notifications

If price alerts are enabled, SQuote may display local Android notifications when a configured quote reaches its alert condition. Notification settings are controlled by Android.

Data retention and deletion

The developer does not retain application data on a server. To delete locally stored SQuote data, clear the application's storage through Android settings or uninstall SQuote.

Data handled by Yahoo Finance is subject to Yahoo Finance's own policies.

Children's privacy

SQuote is not specifically directed toward children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the application's functionality or data handling changes. The current version will be published at this URL.
