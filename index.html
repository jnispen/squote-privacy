SQuote – Stock Tracker Widget Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-26

SQuote – Stock Tracker Widget is a free Android application that displays stock and market quotes in home-screen widgets. The application is provided free of charge and does not require a user account.

Information stored on your device

SQuote stores widget settings locally on your device, including stock or market symbols, refresh-frequency settings, optional price-alert thresholds, and widget display preferences.

This information remains on your device. The developer does not receive or maintain a personal user database containing these settings.

Information sent over the network

To obtain quote information, SQuote sends the requested stock or market symbol to Yahoo Finance's quote service. This request is necessary for the application to retrieve and display market data.

SQuote does not intentionally send your name, email address, contacts, location, account credentials, payment information, or advertising identifier. Normal Internet connection information, such as an IP address and technical request metadata, may be visible to Yahoo Finance or network providers. Their handling of that information is governed by their own privacy policies and terms.

Data collection, sharing, and tracking

The developer does not collect, sell, rent, or share personal user data. SQuote does not maintain application data on a developer-controlled server and does not disclose locally stored widget settings or alert thresholds to third parties.

SQuote does not contain advertising, user accounts, behavioral analytics, usage analytics, or cross-application tracking. The developer does not receive a list of users and does not know which individuals use the application or how they use it.

Market data disclaimer

SQuote displays market data obtained from Yahoo Finance. Quotes and related information may be delayed, incomplete, inaccurate, or temporarily unavailable. They should not be considered real-time market data.

SQuote is provided for general informational purposes only. Nothing displayed by SQuote constitutes financial, investment, legal, tax, or other professional advice. No information in the application is a recommendation, solicitation, endorsement, guarantee, or prediction regarding any security, investment, or trading strategy.

Nothing in the application implies that the developer recommends, endorses, or guarantees any security, company, market, investment, or course of action. You are solely responsible for your own investment decisions. Consider consulting a qualified financial professional before making investment decisions.

SQuote is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Yahoo Finance, any stock exchange, or any company represented by a displayed symbol.

Notifications

If price alerts are enabled, SQuote may display local Android notifications when a configured quote reaches its alert condition. Notification settings are controlled by Android. Notifications are informational and do not constitute financial advice or a recommendation to buy or sell anything.

Data retention and deletion

The developer does not retain application data on a server. To delete locally stored SQuote data, clear the application's storage through Android settings or uninstall SQuote.

Data handled by Yahoo Finance is subject to Yahoo Finance's own policies.

Service availability

SQuote is provided free of charge and on an “as available” basis. No particular availability, accuracy, timeliness, completeness, or uninterrupted operation is guaranteed. Quote retrieval may be affected by network problems, service interruptions, market closures, provider limits, or changes to the external quote service.

Children's privacy

SQuote is not specifically directed toward children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

This policy may be updated when the application's functionality or data handling changes. The current version of the policy will be published at this URL.
